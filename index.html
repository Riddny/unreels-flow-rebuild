What this is

We went through Unreels the way a new user does, then rebuilt the parts we think are leaving money on the table.

Adapty powers subscriptions for 30,000+ apps, so we see what moves conversion and revenue across the market, not inside one app. We pointed that at Unreels: walked your onboarding from the first screen to the paywall, read your App Store listing and its price list, and looked for gaps between what you already have and what you show people.

Your flow is already strong. It asks good questions and has a loader and a payoff. Most of what follows is about making those answers show up later in the flow. There are five ideas. Each is a hypothesis you can A/B test, and each is built into a clickable prototype. The prototype uses your design, not ours.

We did not redesign your app

Every screen in the prototype is yours: the black canvas, the glowing lime button, the photo tiles, the gradient loader bars, the plan cards with “Экономия 89%”. What changes is what those screens say back to the user, plus one extra screen when the paywall closes.

You may have tested some of this

We are working from the outside, so we cannot see your experiment history. If you have already run one of these, tell us and we will go deeper on the rest instead.
